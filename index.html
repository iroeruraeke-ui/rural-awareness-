About Rural Awareness

Rural Awareness gives rural communities a place to report problems with water, roads, health, schools and more, and to share the stories that deserve attention.

How it works

1. You report or share

Send a problem or a story from any phone.

2. We review

Our team checks each submission before it goes public. Your phone number is never shown.

3. You follow progress

Reports move from open to in progress to resolved, with public updates.
